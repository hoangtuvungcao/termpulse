# Community code of conduct

TermPulse welcomes questions, bug reports, technical disagreement and constructive review. Be respectful of other people's time, experience, language and background. Explain concerns with evidence, discuss the work, and give others room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, persistent unwanted contact and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, private connection details, unredacted user data or remote-desktop captures. Do not use issues, discussions or pull requests to advertise unrelated products or to pressure volunteers for immediate support.

Maintainers may ask for edits, hide or remove content, close or lock a thread, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain a moderation decision when that can be done without exposing private information or inviting further abuse. Repeated or severe violations can lead to a ban from project spaces.

For a public concern, use the moderation/report controls on the relevant GitHub content. Do not repeat abusive or private material in a new public thread. The project has not yet designated a separate confidential conduct contact; use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools for sensitive conduct reports. Vulnerabilities belong in the separate private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in project issues, discussions, reviews and other project-managed community spaces. It does not promise continuous moderation or a response deadline.
